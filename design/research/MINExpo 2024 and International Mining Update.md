## Dynamic Power Transfer Systems

### Liebherr Power Rail

**Overview:**
Liebherr's Innovation Lab at MINExpo 2024 showcased pioneering concepts for the future of mining, including a next-generation dynamic power transfer solution: **Liebherr Power Rail**.

**Key Features:**
- Lightweight, single electric rail infrastructure footprint
- Interoperable and minimally invasive side-arm type current collector
- Successfully deployed at **VA Erzberg iron ore mine in Austria**
- Designed to boost productivity and encourage rapid expansion of zero emissions fleets
- Quick installation reduces costs compared to other dynamic options
- Less intrusive on haul roads with **switchback capability (two lanes)**

**Performance:**
- Up to **1.8x faster speeds** on a 10% grade (speed of 5 km / 34 mph)
- Relatively simple installation and relocation
- Intentionally designed for easy maintenance

**Technology:**
Dynamic power transfer is an effective first step towards zero emissions mining sites and beyond traditional overhead trolley assist. Available options include:
- Liebherr Power Rail
- BluVeinXL solution from **BluVein** (joint venture between Australia-based mining innovator Oliber and Quebec-based electric highways developer Evias)
- BluVein refers to its connector as the **BluVein Hammer™**

**Product Range:**
Liebherr continues to offer conventional pantograph trolley solutions across its entire truck product range, including:
- New **T 264 (240 t)** battery-electric truck
- T 236, T 274 (305 t) and T 284 for both AC and diesel engine models

**Active Projects:**
- VA Erzberg (T 236)
- FQM Cobre Panama (T 284)
- FQM Sentinel
- Four T 284 trucks and trolley infrastructure for pilot project at large copper mine (expected 2025)

### Caterpillar DET (Dynamic Energy Transfer)

**Overview:**
Caterpillar recently unveiled its **Dynamic Energy Transfer (DET)** system, described as a fully Caterpillar-developed system that can transfer energy to both diesel-electric and battery-electric mining trucks while they are working around a mine site.

**Key Features:**
- Comprised of integrated elements including a power module
- Converts energy from mine site's power source through electrified rail system
- Transfers energy to truck's powertrain via side-arm collector
- **Highly deployable, mobile solution** customizable to site layouts
- Includes high-speed and curved haul roads
- Can be installed on either side of truck
- Fits multiple truck models
- Suitable for mature or developing sites
- Easily moved or expanded for maximum coverage

**Integration:**
- Integrates with **Cat MineStar™ Command** for haulage solution
- Merges autonomy and electrification technologies
- Provides holistic site solution

**Testing Program:**
Planned for **MINExpo 2024** - BHP will be first customer to trial DET:
- Testing on both battery electric and diesel electric mining trucks at BHP sites
- Result of **over two years** of close collaboration between BHP and Caterpillar
- Trials include validating solution in BHP Iron ore and Copper businesses
- Including **CAT 797** fleet at Escondida, **CAT 798** fleet at Spence, and **CAT 798** fleet at Escondida
- BHP intends to trial Cat DET as integrated system with Cat autonomous solutions

**Demonstration:**
During a visit to Caterpillar's Tucson Proving Grounds (TPG) in Arizona prior to MINExpo, IM Editor Daniel Gleeson witnessed the advanced solution:

**"Drag Race" Test:**
- Two **798 ACs** competed (one with DET system, one diesel-electric only)
- Test conducted on "Tinaja 10" track at the facility
- Haul road developed to showcase DET's speed and grade capabilities
- Seamless transition on and off the DET rails
- **Average 10% incline** (name suggests it eventually stretch to 1.6 km, currently 400m long)
- At TPG, DET system on Tinaja 10 powered mainly by diesel generator sets

**Performance Results:**
The drag race at TPG saw the DET-enabled 798 AC beat its diesel-electric companion to the top of the ramp, with Caterpillar estimating that **1 MW more power** was going into the motors of the 798 AC on the DET line than the diesel-electric-only equivalent.

*Source: Paul Moore, Editorial Director, paul@im-mining.com*

## Battery Electric Mining Trucks

### CAT 793 XE Early Learner Program

**Program Status:**
Caterpillar announced it has achieved the next milestone in the company's battery electric truck development journey, reinforcing its commitment to providing zero-exhaust emissions solutions for customers.

**Current Deployment:**
The next generation of Caterpillar's battery electric Large Mining Trucks have arrived at select customer sites for testing and validation. Caterpillar says it has now completed building and testing **seven Cat® 793 XE Early Learner battery electric trucks** at its Tucson Proving Ground facility in Green Valley, Arizona.

**Program Objectives:**
This marks the second phase of deployment of the company's Early Learner program following the successful demonstration of its first battery electric 793 prototype in November 2022. Caterpillar will also continue testing and validating some of the Early Learner model trucks at its proving ground facility.

**Development History:**
The next generation of these next generation 793 XEs at the Tucson Proving Grounds were showcased to a select group of media including *IM* prior to MINExpo 2024.

**Technical Background:**
Caterpillar also showcased a prototype 70.5 t, a battery electric version of the 70.5-ton payload diesel-powered truck.

**Quote - Denise Johnson, Caterpillar Group President:**
> "We added this Early Learner phase to customer sites earlier than we have before in our traditional product development process. These Early Learner machines will be used to refine requirements, develop processes and validate both the machine and technology designs. Testing these trucks at our customers' sites will provide invaluable feedback for our battery truck program."

**Early Learner Program Launch:**
Caterpillar launched its Early Learner program in 2021 to accelerate its development and validation of Cat battery electric large mining trucks with support from key mining customers and Cat dealers. The company expanded the Early Learner program to include Off-Highway Trucks to support increasing demand from the quarry and aggregates industries.

**Program Purpose:**
One of the primary objectives of the program is for Caterpillar to collaborate more closely with its customers to better understand the impacts of the energy transition on a mine site's people, processes, infrastructure and technology. The Early Learner phase of the program will be key to witnessing those impacts in real world environments. It will also be critical to validate the enhanced design elements of the 793 XE.

**Customer Perspective:**
A customer explains: "In less than two years, we went from retrofitting an existing piece of equipment at our proving ground to designing a ruggedised solution ready for validation in our customers' sites. This was not a small change. Just about everything in the powertrain has been enhanced while still leveraging proven components of our Cat 793 model. With these changes, we still have learning to do with our customers in real-world applications."

**Next Phase:**
Caterpillar's next phase of the Early Learner program will be to integrate multiple electrified trucks at sites, validating the integration of a battery electric fleet with Caterpillar's autonomy and fleet management.

#### Technical Features of HATL Battery Trucks

**Power Battery System:**
- Adopts **LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) core** and **CTP structure form**
- Safe and reliable with high energy density
- **Modularized design concept:**
  - Power supply branch set up as power unit
  - Each power unit independent of each other
  - Number of power units can be increased or reduced according to actual working conditions
  - Amount of power carried by whole vehicle can be adjusted
- Each power unit adopts independent mechanical structure
- Convenient for overall assembly and disassembly
- Improves disassembly and assembly efficiency
- Enhances maintainability of battery system

**Current System Capacity:**
At present, the discharging capacity of the supporting power battery system **exceeds 5 million kWh**, and the system runs stably and reliably.

**Future Development - Higher Voltage Platform:**
HATL will soon launch a higher voltage platform power battery system to match mining trucks with total capacity above 220 tons. Features:
- Does not require high power DC/DC to lift voltage
- Results in higher system efficiency
- Reduced cost and improved reliability
- For underground specific working condition scenarios: launching charging and switching integrated power battery system program
- Will meet various replenishment needs of underground pure electric equipment

**1,000V High-Voltage Platform:**
- Direct-drive motor
- Independent water-cooled main and auxiliary converter program
- Battery bus directly connected to steering and lifting control module
- Effectively reduces weight and volume of system
- Improves efficiency of electric braking
- Reduces weight of cables
- Improves operating efficiency of whole vehicle

**Wide Voltage Fluctuation Range:**
Adapts to dynamic power regulation strategy of power battery system and optimizes energy utilisation rate. Adopting distributed programmable domain control technology to replace traditional centralised low-voltage power distribution architecture, the power distribution structure is optimised and low-voltage wiring harness is reduced.

#### HATL Frame Design

**Frame Engineering:**
Combined with the original fuel vehicle frame equipment layout, HATL analyses the trend of frame load distribution. For the new mechanical structure parts, in the design, the materials selected are the same or similar to the frame materials, the analysis and calculation of the new structural parts are carried out under the actual operating conditions of the vehicle, and the structural lightweight and modularised integrated design is carried out under the premise of guaranteeing the structural strength and taking into consideration the convenience of maintenance.

In the construction, the reasonable welding process is adopted to ensure that there is no damage to the original frame in the process of transformation, and the service life of the transformed vehicle is improved effectively. In the industrial design of the whole vehicle, the principle of 'original vehicle-oriented' is followed, and the original vehicle-industrial load distribution is retained after the transformation.

## Charging Infrastructure

### Stäubli Quick Charging System

**Overview:**
Stäubli Electrical Connectors launched an "industry-leading" **7.5 MW charger** that debuted at MINExpo in Las Vegas. Heavy mining vehicles require high charging power to charge their large-capacity batteries, making them challenging to electrify reliably.

**Challenge:**
High power must be transmitted in the shortest possible time to ensure the mine's expensive equipment comes as close as possible to continuous use.

**QCC-5000 Fast Charging Connector System:**
Further developed specifically for the mining industry to electrify fleets and accelerate transition to zero-emission mining operations.

**Key Features:**
- Allows vehicles to mate with charger automatically
- Drivers no longer have to get out of cab to initiate charging process
- Depending on application, vehicle type and general customer needs: offers variety of integration possibilities
- Can use standard Stäubli guiding system, mounted to robot arm, or fully customised solutions
- Fully charging compatible with already existing QCC-1500 (allowing use of same interface for varying charging power levels)

**Technical Specifications:**
- Charging with up to **4,250 A continuous current** and **5,000 A peak at 1500 V DC**
- **IP55 qualified** with ingress protection from water and dust
- Reliable electrical connection guarantees steady, fast charging
- Reliability and high number of mating cycles guarantee long service life even in harshest environments
- Will significantly reduce costs for customers across complete product lifecycle

**Thermal Management:**
Higher charging power means managing heat. By cooling its cables and the charging pin and verifying performance in extensive test sessions, Stäubli achieved 7.5 MW charging capability.

**Safety:**
- Automated connector on QCC-5000 has safety in mind
- Touch protection keeps contacts from water and dust
- System reveals charging contacts at end of connection process when pin and socket are fully connected
- Current manual systems feature unprotected contacts

**Standards Compliance:**
Quick-Charging Connector System built for mining industry aligns with **SAE J3105/3 electric vehicle charging standard**. This open standard, encompassing design of charging pins and sockets, paves the way for various suppliers to provide compatible solutions.

**System Design:**
Designed to ensure well-defined number of low-resistance contact points which enables continuous contact with contact surface, resulting in low and constant electrical resistance at contact points.

## Key Takeaways

### Industry Trends

1. **Multiple electrification pathways** are being pursued:
   - Pure battery-electric (HATL, CAT 793 XE)
   - Dynamic power transfer/trolley assist (Liebherr, CAT DET)
   - Hybrid battery + fuel cell (Anglo American)

2. **Testing and validation phase** is critical:
   - CAT 793 XE Early Learner program demonstrates industry moving from concept to field testing
   - HATL has achieved operational success with **13,131 hours** on first HAT-R100E

3. **Chinese manufacturers** (HATL/CATL) are leading in smaller battery-electric truck deployment:
   - 60-100 ton class trucks in production
   - Plans for 220+ ton class development

4. **Charging infrastructure** is rapidly evolving:
   - Stäubli: 7.5 MW automated charging
   - ABB: Robotic automated connection devices
   - Both systems designed for harsh mining environments

5. **Payload classes matter:**
   - 60-100 ton class: Multiple operational trucks (HATL)
   - 200+ ton class: Still in early development/testing (CAT 793 XE)
   - Gap exists between proven technology (90t) and largest trucks needed (300t+)
